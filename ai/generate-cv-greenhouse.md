## Emphasis for this posting: clean core fields

This posting's system may extract skills, job titles, years of experience, employment dates and employer names from the CV, and may not use what the candidate types into the application form. The ground rule still applies:
NOTHING here allows a claim the source CV does not support.

- Write `role` in full: "Senior Account Executive", not "Sr. Account Exec". Expand an abbreviation only when its meaning is certain.
- Write `company` as the source CV writes it, keeping any "Inc.", "Co.", "LTD" or "LLC". Do not add one the source does not have, and do not shorten the name.
- Give every experience its `start_date` and `end_date`, in the same format throughout.
- Keep each field to its own content: no dates or locations inside `role`, no job title inside `company`.
- Make sure the skills and experience this posting asks for appear in the CV itself, since the application form may not count.
