This posting's system may check each qualification individually against the parsed CV, and required qualifications weigh more than preferred ones. The ground rule still applies: nothing here allows a claim the source CV does not support.

- List the posting's required qualifications for yourself, then its preferred ones.
- For each one the source CV supports, make sure a bullet, a skill entry or `profile_text` answers it clearly, in the posting's own wording where the CV supports that wording.
- Put the evidence for required qualifications ahead of the evidence for preferred ones, inside each experience and in `skills`.
